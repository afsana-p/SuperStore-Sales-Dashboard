Power BI dashboard with sales, profit, delivery analysis and a sales forecast for the SuperStore dataset.

# SuperStore Sales Dashboard

## About the Project
This dashboard analyses sales, profit and delivery performance of a Super Store across categories, regions and states. It shows how sales and profit change over time and which states perform best on the map. It also includes a 15-day sales forecast to help plan future demand. Overall, it turns raw order data into clear information for business decisions.

## Key Metrics
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders (Quantity)
- Average Delivery Days

## Report Pages
- **SSS Dashboard**: KPIs, sales by category, sub-category and ship mode, sales map by state, profit and sales trends, Region slicer
- **SSS Forecast**: 15-day sales forecast by category

## Key Insights
- **Overall:** 2M sales, 22K orders, 175K profit, 4 days average shipping.
- **Category:** Office Supplies leads (0.64M), then Technology (0.47M) and Furniture (0.45M).
- **Sub-Category:** Phones, Chairs and Binders are the top sellers.
- **State:** California is first (0.34M), then New York (0.19M) and Texas (0.12M).
- **Segment:** Consumer 48%, Corporate 33%, Home Office 19%.
- **Payment:** COD 43%, Online 35%, Cards 22%.
- **Ship Mode:** Standard Class is the most used (0.91M).
- **Trend:** Sales and profit peak from September to December.
- **Forecast:** The 15-day forecast shows daily sales of about 3K at the end of the period.
