# SuperStore Sales Dashboard
An interactive Power BI report on SuperStore sales, profit and shipping performance, including a sales forecast.

## Objective
Understand sales and profit by category, region and customer segment, and predict future sales trends.

## Key Metrics
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders (Quantity)
- Average Delivery Days

## Report Pages
- **SSS Dashboard** – KPIs, sales by category / sub-category / ship mode, payment mode and segment split, sales map by state, profit and sales trends, Region slicer
- **SSS Forecast** – Sales forecast by category over time
- **Insights** – Summary of key findings
